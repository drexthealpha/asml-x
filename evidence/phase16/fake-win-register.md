# Task 16.4: the fake-win register, checked

33 fake wins are named in TASKS.md. **33 have a claim in the evidence
chain for that subtask; 0 do not.**

## What this register can and cannot tell you

It checks that every named fake win has a claim covering its subtask, and it prints the claim
so a reader can judge the refusal themselves.

**It cannot decide whether a refusal is convincing.** That is a judgement, and a script that
pretended to make it would itself be this task's fake win: a green tick asserting that thirty
traps were avoided, generated by the same process that might have fallen into them. So what
follows reports COVERAGE and shows the text. Correctness is left where it belongs.
