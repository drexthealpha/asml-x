## Why risk-engine specifically
  It is the only crate whose failure is unbounded. A wrong score costs a bad trade; a
  wrong limit check costs the cap. It is also the crate most amenable to mutation:
  pure integer arithmetic, no clock reads, no floats, so every mutant is deterministic
  and a survivor cannot be explained away as flakiness.

## Bounds, and why each
  --timeout 90        a mutant that hangs is a survivor by timeout, not by merit
  --in-place          this box has 4.9 GB RAM; cargo-mutants' default copies the tree
                      per job and that is what makes it thrash here
  (serial)            --in-place is INHERENTLY serial: cargo-mutants rejects
                      '--in-place with --jobs' outright, because one working tree
                      cannot hold two mutants at once. My first invocation passed
                      both and the tool refused, correctly.
